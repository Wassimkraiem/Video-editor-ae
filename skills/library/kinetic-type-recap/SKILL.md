# kinetic-type-recap

Confidence through restraint. Few words, perfectly timed, with space around them. Build it as one
HyperFrames composition.

## Rules measured from premium recap videos

- **Type size:** cap height about 6-7% of the frame height (on 1920 px: 120-140 px font), weight
  600, one family (the brand font, else Inter / SF-like sans). Headlines may go to 10-12% for the
  single biggest word of the video.
- **Shot length:** median about 1.9 s; range 0.8-3.5 s. A "shot" is one word group, one product
  visual or one colour field.
- **Cuts on the beat:** every change lands on a strong beat of the music; if there is speech,
  on word onsets.
- **Words are rationed:** 1-4 words per shot, never a sentence on screen. Numbers get their own shot.
- **Most frames are not text:** alternate text shots with visuals (the product, a UI detail,
  footage, or a full-bleed colour field with one shape).

## Structure

1. Cold open: one word or a number, alone, centred (0-2 s).
2. 3-6 "chapters": a chapter title shot -> 2-4 detail shots (feature, number, visual) -> on to the next.
3. Finale: the product/brand name big, then one line (date, CTA or tagline).

With speech, captions are replaced by these shots: show only the key words of each sentence.

## Look

- Backgrounds: pure black #000 or pure white #FFF, switching between chapters; or full-bleed
  colour fields from the brand kit (one per chapter).
- Text is white on black, black on white, or white on colour. No outlines, no shadows, no boxes.
- Accent colour only on numbers or the one key word of a chapter (a gradient fill is allowed).
- Product/UI visuals large and centred, on the same background, with generous margins.

## Motion

- Words: either a clean cut (preferred), or a short rise (y 40 -> 0, 0.35 s, power3.out) with a
  blur 8 -> 0 px. Letters never bounce.
- Accelerated type moments (once or twice): words scale from 0.9 to 1.0 over the whole shot for a
  slow push, or a word grows to fill the frame on a drop.
- Numbers count up fast (0.6 s) and stop exactly on a beat.
- Visual shots: slow push-in 1.0 -> 1.05 over the shot.

## Sound

A rhythmic, clean track drives everything; no SFX except one soft hit on the finale.

## Check

No shot holds more than 4 words; most changes on beats; one font family; at least as many visual
shots as text shots; ends on the name and one line.
