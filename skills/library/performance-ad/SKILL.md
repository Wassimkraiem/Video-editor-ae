# performance-ad

A direct-response ad. Every second earns the next one. Build it as one HyperFrames composition.

## Structure (scale the timings to the video's length)

| Beat | 15 s | 30 s | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2 s | 0-3 s | The viewer's problem or a bold claim as a BIG headline (2-6 words), moving from frame 1. Never a logo. |
| Problem | 2-5 s | 3-8 s | Make the pain concrete: one image of it (a messy list, a red error, a price tag, a clock). |
| Solution | 5-11 s | 8-20 s | The product doing the job, on screen: UI card, product shot or the speaker using it. One feature, one result. |
| Proof | 11-13 s | 20-25 s | One number, a star rating, a short quote or a logo row. Skip if the script has none; never invent numbers. |
| CTA | last 2-3 s | last 3-5 s | One action ("Download free", "Comment LINK", "Shop now") on a button-shaped card + logo if given. |

With a talking-head clip, follow what the speaker says: put each beat's graphic on the words that
carry it. Without footage, write the on-screen lines yourself from the user's ideas or script.

## Layout (1080x1920)

- Safe zone: nothing important in the top 250 px, the bottom 320 px or the right 120 px.
- Hook headline: centred at 30-40% height, 120-150 px font, max 2 lines, heavy weight.
- Captions (talking head): lower third (y about 1250-1450 px), 2-4 words per line, one keyword in
  the accent colour. Hide them while a full-screen card is up.
- Cards (UI, product, proof): 820-900 px wide, rounded 36-48 px corners, soft shadow; enter from
  the side the eye is already looking at.

## Look

- Brand kit colours if given (first = accent for keyword, CTA button, highlights). Otherwise:
  near-black #0B0B0F or off-white #F7F7F5 background + ONE saturated accent.
- One heavy sans for headlines (Montserrat 900, Inter 800, Archivo Black), one clean sans for text.
- Problem beat may use a desaturated or red-tinted look; the solution switches to the brand
  colours: the colour change itself tells the story.

## Motion

- Hook: words punch in (scale 1.25 -> 1, 0.25 s, back.out) or slam from below with a camera shake
  of 6-10 px; something moves within the first 10 frames.
- Cuts and card changes on word onsets; entrances 0.3-0.4 s (power3.out), exits 0.2 s.
- Talking head: punch-in to 1.12 on the hook and on the result line; a quick zoom-out on the CTA.
- One transition type for the whole ad (hard cut or whip).
- CTA button: scale in, then a soft pulse (1 -> 1.04, 0.6 s, yoyo) until the end; a tap dot on it.

## Sound

Riser into the hook word, a pop/click on each card, a whoosh on the transition into the solution,
a soft "success" chime on the proof, silence or a hit on the CTA. Never louder than the voice.

## Check before answering

Hook in the first 2 s without a logo; product visible by ~40% of the duration; one feature, one
result; real proof only; one CTA; everything inside the safe zone; readable on mute.
