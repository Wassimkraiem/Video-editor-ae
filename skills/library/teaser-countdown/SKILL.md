# teaser-countdown

Show less than everything. Curiosity is the product. Build it as one HyperFrames composition;
footage is optional (a speaker can be graded dark and shown in short glimpses).

## Structure (30 s; scale to length)

1. **Black + typed line (0-4 s):** a question or a provocation typed character by character with
   a blinking caret ("What if your inbox answered itself?").
2. **Fragments (4-20 s):** 4-7 glimpses, 1.5-3 s each: a cropped corner of the UI, a product edge
   catching light, a single icon highlighted in a grid, one word of a feature. Never the whole thing.
3. **Build-up (20-26 s):** fragments get faster (0.5-0.8 s each), the music rises.
4. **Reveal-but-not-quite (last 4 s):** the name (or a silhouette/logo shape), the date or a
   countdown, and the CTA: "Join the waitlist", "Comment EARLY", "Notify me".

## Look

- Background near-black #050507; vignette at the edges.
- Rim light: product/UI pieces lit by a thin bright edge in the accent colour (a gradient border
  plus a 40-80 px glow); everything else falls into shadow (a mask gradient to black).
- Typed text: monospace or the brand font, 56-72 px, white at 90%, left-aligned, caret in the accent.
- Countdown: big tabular numbers (DD : HH : MM or "10.21"), thin weight, the separators pulse.
- Brand kit colour = the rim light and caret; nothing else gets colour.

## Motion

- Slow: camera drifts (pan 20-40 px, push 1.0 -> 1.06) across each fragment.
- Fragments appear with a light sweep (a bright gradient bar crossing the shape, 0.8 s) or a
  flicker-on (opacity steps 0 -> 0.6 -> 0.2 -> 1 over 0.25 s).
- Build-up: hard cuts on beats with brief white flashes (2 frames) between them.
- The final name: assembled from a blur (24 -> 0 px) and letter spacing collapsing (0.4em -> 0).

## Sound

Low drone or pulse building to a hit; typing clicks under the typed line; a deep impact on the
reveal; silence for the last half second before the CTA.

## Check

Nothing shown in full until the end; one question or provocation up front; the date or countdown
present; exactly one CTA; mostly dark frames.
