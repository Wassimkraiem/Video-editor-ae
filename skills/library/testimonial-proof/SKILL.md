# testimonial-proof

Credibility comes from the person; the design only frames their words. Keep it light and human.
Build it as one HyperFrames composition over the speaker's footage.

## Structure

1. **Result hook (0-3 s):** their single strongest result line as a big headline over the footage
   ("We doubled our orders in 30 days"), taken word for word from the transcript.
2. **Who (3-6 s):** lower-third name card: name, role/company or city, optional small avatar.
   Stay up 3 s.
3. **Story (middle):** clean captions; 2-3 of their best sentences become quote cards (large
   quotation mark, the line, their name small) for 2-4 s each.
4. **Proof (if the transcript has it):** a number card (before -> after counting), stars (5 drawn
   in sequence), or a row of review snippets. Only numbers they actually said.
5. **Soft CTA (last 3 s):** "Try it yourself", "Link in bio", product name/logo.

## Look

- Warm and natural: keep the footage as is (no heavy grade); light vignette only.
- Cards: off-white #FAF7F2 or white with 92% opacity, dark text #1A1A1A, radius 28 px, soft
  shadow; or the brand kit's colours (first = quote marks, stars, numbers).
- Type: a friendly serif for quotes (Fraunces, Playfair Display, DM Serif) at 56-72 px; a clean
  sans for names and captions.
- Captions: lower third, 3-5 words, white with a soft shadow (no heavy box). Hide them under quote
  cards.

## Motion

- Gentle: fades and rises of 24-40 px, 0.5 s, power2.out. No shakes, no flashes.
- Quote card: slides up from the bottom while the footage softly scales to 0.94 and blurs 6 px
  behind it; reversed when it leaves.
- Stars draw one after another (0.08 s stagger) with a tiny scale pop.
- At most 2 slow push-ins (1.0 -> 1.06) on emotional lines.

## Sound

Soft acoustic or warm piano bed, low under the voice; a light chime on stars and numbers only.

## Check

Their words are never changed; the result is in the first 3 s; name card shown early; proof only
from what they said; calm motion; one soft CTA.
