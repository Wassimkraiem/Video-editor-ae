# three-step-promo

Simplicity is the message: anyone can do this in three moves. Build it as one HyperFrames
composition, no footage required (a talking-head clip can play in a rounded card on the steps).

## Structure (for 20 s; scale for 15 s)

| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-5 s | The pain or the promise in one line ("Approvals take days?" / "Ship in one click"). Kinetic: 2-3 words at a time. |
| Step 1 | 5-8.5 s | Giant word ("Upload.") + the UI doing it. |
| Step 2 | 8.5-12 s | Giant word ("Share.") + the UI doing it. |
| Step 3 | 12-15 s | Giant word ("Approve.") + the result (check, number, happy state). |
| Outro | 15-20 s | The three words together in one line, product name/logo, CTA. |

Take the three steps from the user's ideas or script. Each step word is ONE verb with a full stop.

## Look

- Step words: 220-280 px, heaviest weight (Archivo Black, Montserrat 900, Inter 900), tight
  letter-spacing (-0.03em), left-aligned at x 90 px, y about 520 px.
- A step counter "1/3" small and light near the word.
- UI card under the word: 900 px wide, rounded 32 px, showing the action in 1-2 moves.
- Two-colour scheme: background + accent (brand kit first colour). Alternate the background
  between the two colours on each step (inverting the text), which makes the rhythm visible.

## Motion

- Hook words: hard-cut in on beats, slight scale overshoot (1.08 -> 1, 0.2 s).
- Step word: slides in from the left with a fast expo.out (0.35 s) while the previous one exits
  left; the colour flip happens on the same frame.
- UI action: one clear move per step (file card flying into a dropzone, a share sheet opening,
  a stamp "Approved" landing with a small shake).
- Outro: the three words line up ("Upload. Share. Approve.") with a stagger of 0.12 s.

## Sound

Punchy beat; one hit per step word, a whoosh on each colour flip, a stamp/thud on the final
step, a clean hit on the logo.

## Check

Exactly three steps, one verb each; the pain or promise lands in the first 5 s; the UI shows each
action, not just a static screen; ends on the product name and one CTA.
